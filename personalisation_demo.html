How it works. A transparent weighted-evidence scorer reads only observable session signals (never identity or lookalike cohorts), sums evidence toward each of four intents, and serves the matching layout. When the winning intent clears < 42% confidence it falls back to a neutral home page — getting personalisation wrong is worse than not personalising.
Caveat. Presets are real rows from a 1,000-session synthetic dataset; offline accuracy is 97.5% but optimistic. Time_of_Day is ignored (pure noise) and browsing-sequence signals aren't captured yet — both noted as next steps.
